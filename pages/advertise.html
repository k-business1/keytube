❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How long does approval take? ▼
Our team reviews all ad requests within 1–2 business days. You will receive an email at the address you provided with our decision and pricing details.
How much does advertising cost? ▼
Pricing depends on ad type, placement, duration and targeting. Banner ads start from $20/month. We discuss final pricing with you after reviewing your request.
What types of ads are accepted? ▼
We accept image banners, short video ads, text/link ads and sponsored content placements. Ads must not contain illegal, adult or misleading content.
Can I track my ad performance? ▼
Yes! Once your ad is approved and active, we provide weekly reports with views, clicks and engagement data for your campaign.
How do I pay? ▼
We accept Mobile Money (MTN, Airtel), bank transfer and PayPal. Payment details are sent after your ad is approved.
