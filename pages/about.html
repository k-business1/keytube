🎬 What is KEYTUBE?

KEYTUBE is a next-generation video streaming platform designed to bring entertainment from every corner of the world to your screen. Whether you love Hollywood blockbusters, Bollywood drama, Chinese series, African films, cartoons, music videos or breaking news — KEYTUBE has it all.

🌍 Our Mission

We believe great content should be accessible to everyone, everywhere. Our mission is to democratize entertainment by giving creators a platform to share their stories and giving viewers a seamless, enjoyable streaming experience.

✨ Key Features

  • 📱 Watch on any device — mobile, tablet, or desktop
  • ⬇ Download videos for offline viewing
  • 📋 Save videos to your personal playlist
  • ❤️ Like and comment on your favourite content
  • 👥 Follow your favourite channels and creators
  • 🎬 Upload and share your own videos
  • 💰 Earn money from your content once you reach 1,000 followers
  • 🔔 Get notified about new content from channels you follow
  • 🔍 Powerful search across all categories

📜 Our Story

KEYTUBE was born from a simple idea: the world's best stories deserve a global stage. Founded in Kigali, Rwanda, we set out to build a streaming platform that celebrates African and global content equally — giving every creator and viewer a home.
