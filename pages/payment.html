❓ Payment FAQ

Can I cancel my subscription?▼

Yes, you can cancel anytime from your Profile settings. You'll continue to have access until the end of your billing period. No questions asked.

Is my payment information secure?▼

Absolutely. All payments are processed through encrypted connections. We never store your card details directly on our servers.

Do you offer refunds?▼

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for new subscriptions. If you're not satisfied, contact us at contact@keytube.com within 7 days of your purchase.

Can I switch plans?▼

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

How do Mobile Money payments work?▼

Enter your MTN, Airtel, or M-Pesa number and tap Pay. You'll receive a prompt on your phone to confirm the payment. Enter your Mobile Money PIN to complete the transaction.
